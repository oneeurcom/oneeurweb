Mobile app

The OneEur wallet.

A simple wallet for iOS and Android. Send and receive OneEur in milliseconds, pay with a QR code, and watch your own chain grow.

Features

Everything a wallet needs, nothing else.

Create or restore

Create a new wallet in seconds, or restore one you already have with its recovery key. Your key is made on your phone and stays there.

Send in milliseconds

Enter an address and an amount on the keypad, add a note if you like, and tap Send. The payment is confirmed in milliseconds.

Receive and scan

Show a QR code with your address and, if you want, an amount. Scan it in the app or with the phone’s own camera to open Send already filled in.

Money just arrives

Payments to you are collected automatically, and your balance and activity update live as blocks are confirmed.

See your own chain

My chain shows your wallet’s blocks, linked one after another, with the newest glowing gold.

Names for addresses

Give addresses names like “Mom” or “Coffee shop”. They are kept only on your phone.

Getting started

Three steps to your first payment.

  1. 01

    Get the app

    Download the OneEur wallet for iOS or Android from [where to get it].

  2. 02

    Create your wallet

    Tap Create wallet and choose a 6-digit PIN. Your key is made on the phone and encrypted with the PIN.

  3. 03

    Back up your recovery key

    In Settings, tap Copy recovery key and keep it somewhere safe and offline. It is the only way to restore your wallet.

Safe by design

Locked with your PIN.

Your key stays on the phone

The wallet’s secret is encrypted with your PIN and stored in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. The app asks for the PIN when it opens, after a minute in the background, and before anything sensitive. More about security →

Removing the app removes the wallet

Uninstalling the app or tapping Remove wallet deletes the key from the phone. Make sure your recovery key is backed up first.
