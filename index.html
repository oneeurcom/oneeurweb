One chain per wallet

A pays B: only A’s and B’s chains get a new block

Each wallet keeps its own chain of transactions. A payment only updates the sender’s and receiver’s chains, so it never waits for anyone else and completes in milliseconds.

Mirrored everywhere

A full copy on every holder

Every wallet chain is mirrored to many different chain holders. Each holder keeps a full copy. A holder that was offline catches up from the others automatically, and balances can still be read even if some holders are down.
