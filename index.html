A pays B: only A’s and B’s chains get a new block

Each wallet keeps its own chain of transactions. A payment only updates the sender’s and receiver’s chains, so it never waits for anyone else and completes in milliseconds.

Mirrored everywhere

A full copy on every holder

Every wallet chain is mirrored to many different chain holders. Each holder keeps a full copy. A holder that was offline catches up from the others automatically, and balances can still be read even if some holders are down.

For business

Ready for corporate needs.

Settled at checkout

A payment is confirmed in milliseconds, so you know it has arrived before the customer leaves the counter or the page.

Simple JSON API

Connect your own systems with a small HTTPS API. No API keys to manage: every payment is signed by your own wallet.

Invoice references

Every payment can carry an invoice or order number, signed into both the sender’s and receiver’s chains, so bookkeeping can be matched automatically.

Live notifications

Follow a live stream of confirmed payments and react the moment money comes in: ship the order, unlock the service, print the receipt.

Your own infrastructure

Run a chain holder in your own data centre and keep a full copy of every chain, with no dependence on a third party.

Full audit trail

Every transaction is signed, permanent and visible in the explorer, giving auditors a complete, verifiable history.
